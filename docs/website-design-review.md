# VIP Homes website design review

Reviewed against the existing company vision, portfolio content and supplied VIP Creative Homes material on 30 September 2026.

## Overall assessment

The architectural imagery, serif headings and restrained warm palette provide a useful foundation. The main weakness was the story: the homepage read primarily as a Bliss In The Woods promotion, while the builder's vision was long and the completed work was hard to find. A stronger builder website makes the relationship between intention, design and execution easy to understand.

## Changes implemented

- Project detail pages now follow About → Gallery → What Makes It Special → Location → Enquiry. The dedicated VIP Creative Homes experience retains its villa-plan story and places its home-experience features after the plans.
- The homepage introduces the company with a builder message, features its upcoming development and then connects a brief company introduction to ongoing and completed projects.
- Indus Homes has a dedicated completed-work feature on the homepage, using the existing project photos as evidence of execution.
- The founder section now leads with the company's vision, mission and design belief, then explains the project approach and founder background. Copy is shorter and focused on what the vision means for a resident.
- Repeated homepage galleries and the extra BIW-only amenities section have been removed from the homepage. Project-specific galleries and amenities remain on their own project pages.
- Portfolio counts come from project data. They no longer imply that the scale of a partner project's surrounding eco-tourism park represents the builder's own portfolio size.
- The general enquiry form now prepares a message to the confirmed VIP Homes WhatsApp number. It no longer reports an enquiry as received when nothing was sent. It includes a project selector, visible labels and phone validation.
- The homepage location section covers the upcoming, ongoing and completed developments as well as the office, with direct map links.
- Highlight sections use a readable grid rather than a long pinned horizontal scroll. Gallery captions are visible on touch screens; additional photos and existing project videos remain available on demand.
- Gallery viewers use native modal dialogs with Escape and arrow-key navigation. The shared header, menu and back-to-top control stay outside the transformed scrolling content.
- The navigation prioritises Our Vision, Our Projects and VIP Creative Homes. Closed drawer links are excluded from keyboard interaction; the open drawer supports keyboard focus and Escape.
- Footer contrast and project links have been improved. Brand text is consistently VIP Homes. Each standard project page also has its own search and sharing title, description and canonical URL.
- Farm Hills' stock landscape images are identified as illustrative, rather than being described as project-site photos. The completed project's old starting-price statistic has been replaced with its completion status.
- The homepage's artificial loading delay has been removed. Its main message is immediately visible. Native scrolling improves link, form and dialog reliability while retaining subtle visual reveals. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Best next additions from the builder

1. Actual Farm Hills site photos or an approved master layout. This is the highest-priority visual gap; stock scenery cannot demonstrate the development itself.
2. A professional founder portrait and a short approved founder statement or video. This would add a human point of contact to the builder story.
3. Dated project-progress photos and approved milestone descriptions. A dated construction story is stronger evidence of execution than generic quality claims.
4. For VIP Creative Homes, a confirmed community master plan and amenity schedule. The supplied larger layout is not treated as the confirmed 10-acre community plan.
5. Before a public launch, confirm legacy project availability, approval references and travel-time claims with the builder. The site currently retains existing source information; it has not independently verified those details.
6. If the business needs automatic lead capture and follow-up reporting, connect a CRM or form backend. Current enquiries are deliberately completed by the visitor through WhatsApp.

## Validation

Production build and browser checks are recorded in the implementation handoff. The changes are local and require deployment before appearing on the public domain.
